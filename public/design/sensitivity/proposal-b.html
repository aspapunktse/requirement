Sensitivity Levels · Proposal B

Scale

A level is a position on a scale of five. The label pill is followed by a five-segment meter, so how high a System sits is visible next to its name. The Systems list filters with a histogram of the scale, owners climb a ladder from Public to Critical Sensitivity (and see how many Controls each rung brings in), and Admins edit the whole scale with a live preview of all five steps in light and dark.
